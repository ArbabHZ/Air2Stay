Inputs and assumptions

FieldMeaning and unitWhere it comes from
Departure country / originKeyCountry where the flight starts, not traveller nationality or a specific airport.Supplied flight records, or a country selected for a proposed new route.
MonthMonth of year, 1–12. No launch year is selected.User selection. Weeks = days ÷ 7; February uses 28.25 days.
Past flight seatsAverage recorded seats for the selected country and month.Bundled model data. New routes start with zero seats.
Extra flights each weekWhole flights added to or removed from the schedule, −20 to +20.User assumption, multiplied by seats per flight and weeks in the month.
Seats per flightPassenger seats on each added or removed flight, 30–600.Historical or comparison-market starting value; adjustable.
Flight occupancy / loadFactorShare of seats filled, 30–100%.Flight-record starting estimate or chosen scenario assumption. Source occupancy above 100% is capped; trip-ending share is rebased on the same passenger count. Not hotel-room occupancy.
Trip-ending share / p2pShareShare of passengers whose air journey ends in Abu Dhabi, 5–100%.Flight-record starting estimate or comparison-market assumption. May include returning residents.
Hotel use / conversionRateShare of trip-ending passengers assumed to check into hotels, 5–90%.Calibrated from 2022–2024 records only, with full precision retained; adjustable. A country-specific observed conversion rate is not available.
Average stayGuest nights per hotel check-in, 1–10 nights.Weighted visitor stay estimates, or the user's assumption.
Event upliftExtra hotel demand, 0–50%.User assumption. Applied to hotel check-ins and nights, not seats. It is not a measured event effect.
Visitor mixEstimated nationality shares, normalised to sum to 100%.Flight and hotel aggregates, optionally edited. Allocates the same total; does not change the historical baseline.
Comparison countriesUp to three markets with weights, or one manually selected market.Geographic starting heuristic using the same month. It does not prove demand or similar travel behaviour.
Route purposeMixed, holidays, business or family visits.User label. Only explicitly applied hotel-use and stay assumptions change results.

Results

Scenario seats = max(0, past flight seats + extra weekly flights × seats per flight × weeks). Passengers = seats × flight occupancy. Trip-ending passengers = passengers × trip-ending share. Hotel check-ins = trip-ending passengers × hotel use × (1 + event uplift). Guest nights = hotel check-ins × average stay.

Baseline: an estimate based on past flight records and starting hotel assumptions, not an observed route hotel total. Change: scenario minus baseline. Compare: isolates extra flights by comparing the proposal with zero added flights under the same assumptions. These two changes can differ when other assumptions are edited.

Guest nights per 1,000 added seats: added-flight guest-night effect ÷ added seats × 1,000; unavailable without positive added seats. Guest nights count people, not rooms.

Possible range: historical multipliers around the scenario. It is a planning guide, not a calibrated probability or new-route accuracy claim. Matching records: amount of past route/month information, not certainty.

Accuracy scores: the Check past results page separately reports the live constant-rate chain, optional seasonal comparison, and hybrid comparison on held-out monthly aggregate hotel arrivals; the separate daily test concerns daily hotel guests. Neither validates an individual new-route hotel-night prediction.

Proposal workflow

Red means a check is overdue or a rule identifies a concern. Amber means information is missing, pending or needs review after assumptions changed. Green means a user recorded evidence for the exact current assumptions. Read each reason: these colours are not route approval or accuracy scores. Owner names, due dates, comments, evidence and decisions are user records, not independently verified facts.

Traceability

Calculation: engine.mjs; comparisons: decision_tools.mjs. See the current proposal's Details page for exact inputs.
