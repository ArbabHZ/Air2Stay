Example

An Australian passport holder may arrive on a flight from London. The flight file says United Kingdom. The hotel file says Australia. The bridge estimates that relationship instead of forcing the two labels to match.

One product, two connected calculations

Scenario Studio first calculates the total hotel check-ins and guest nights. Country Bridge then divides that same total among likely nationalities. The bridge does not create or remove any demand.

Share of hotel check-ins

The estimated part of the selected route's hotel check-ins assigned to each nationality. All 45 shares add to 100%.

Top nationality weights

The ten largest estimated shares. The other 35 nationalities remain in the calculation even when they are not visible in the table.

Planner override

A user can replace the estimated shares with better evidence. Air2Stay then normalizes all shares to 100% and recalculates the nationality view.

Where the black line comes from

It is not invented. It is the New Arrivals column in the supplied data international_train.xlsx, added across nationalities and days to make one total for each month.

Where the transparent line comes from

For each exam month, supplied P2P passengers are multiplied by a hotel-conversion rate learned from the older training months. That is the same visible logic used by Scenario Studio.

What WMAPE means

WMAPE stands for Weighted Mean Absolute Percentage Error. Add the sizes of all mistakes, then divide by the total actual check-ins. Lower is better. About 7% means about seven check-ins of total mistake per one hundred actual check-ins.

What the daily stay error tests

This is a separate test. It predicts the supplied daily Guests values by nationality for May–July 2025 after learning from earlier days. It checks the hotel-stay part; it does not create the black monthly line.

Known data limits

  • Flight country means the place where the airplane departed.
  • Hotel nationality means the guest's passport nationality.
  • The supplied files do not include purpose of travel, aircraft type, room inventory or events.
  • The model uses monthly flight totals because 2022 has a different date grain.

Security and privacy

  • The prototype runs locally and does not send the supplied data to an external service.
  • The standalone HTML contains derived route baselines, validation totals and bridge weights, not the original row-level workbooks.
  • The Country Bridge estimates patterns across groups. It does not identify or track an individual traveler.
  • Do not publish the original competition data or planner overrides without DCT approval and a named data owner.
