What if fewer visitors stay in hotels?

Stress trials lower flight fullness by 10 percentage points, hotel use by 5 points, and stay length by half a night, within the control limits. They are hypothetical tests, not confidence levels.

How the visitor estimate works
Why the countries differ

Someone may fly from London but hold an Australian passport. Flight records show where the trip started; hotel records show the guest’s nationality. Air2Stay uses both to estimate who may be visiting.

One estimate, shown two ways

First, Air2Stay estimates hotel check-ins and nights. Then it splits that same estimate across visitor nationalities. The split does not change the total.

How the simple estimate works

It starts with passengers whose flight ends in Abu Dhabi, then estimates how many check into hotels.

How to read the difference

It shows how far an estimate was from the recorded total. A smaller difference means a closer estimate. For example, about 7% means roughly seven arrivals of difference for every 100 recorded arrivals.

What the records don’t tell us

  • The departure country is where the flight took off, not necessarily where a traveller lives.
  • The hotel nationality is based on the guest’s passport, not where the flight started.
  • The files don’t show why people travelled, which aircraft they used, how many hotel rooms were available, or which events took place.
  • Because the flight records cover different time periods, the app compares monthly totals.

About privacy and sharing

  • The planner runs locally.
  • When Gemini is enabled in Chat, open-ended questions send your question, recent chat, plan summary and built-in check statuses to Google.
  • Proposal comments, evidence and decisions stay in this browser. Assigned owners are not notified and messages are drafts only.
  • It uses totals for groups, not names or details about individual travellers.
  • Exported files include your plan assumptions and any edited visitor mix. Proposal-history downloads also include your saved notes.
  • Guide me explains local storage, optional usage counts and feedback. Share competition data only with people who are allowed to receive it.
